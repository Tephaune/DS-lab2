In the delimiter checker and the postfix evaluator, each of the stack elements represent an opening delimiter like "(", "[", or "{" and it still has to be closed off. The most recent delimter that's been pushed is necessary because a closing delimiter has to match the closest opening one (that's unfinished). Each algorithm needs the most recently pushed item rather than the oldest one because in the postfix calculator, every stack item is a number that is just waiting to be closed by an operator. The most recently added numbers have to come first because an operator works with the last two numbers that were added. Like in "5 3 -", "3" is popped first and used as the right number, then 5 is popped as the left number, which would leave us with "5 - 3 = 2". That's why the items that are most recently pushed are needed in both algorithms.
